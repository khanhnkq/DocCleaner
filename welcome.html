Step 1 of 3

Open any Studocu or Scribd Document

Navigate to any document page on Studocu or Scribd. DocCleaner activates automatically in the background without requiring any account login.

Tip Look for the navy & lime "Format for Print" button fixed at the bottom-right corner of the page.
Step 2 of 3

Click the Button to Launch Clean Reader

Clicking the floating button strips clutter, eliminates promotional overlays, and assembles the document into centered, distraction-free A4 sheets.

Tip The top Reader Bar stays on screen while reading. Press Esc or click "Exit Clean View" whenever you want to close it.
Step 3 of 3

Save or Print Clean A4 PDF

Click "Print / Save PDF (A4)" in the top bar. Chrome's print dialog will open with pages already centered and mathematically scaled to A4 standard dimensions.

Tip In the Chrome print dialog, set Margins to "None" and ensure "Background graphics" is enabled for the best output quality.
