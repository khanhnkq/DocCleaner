Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions answered about DocCleaner and print formatting.

Is DocCleaner really 100% free to use?

Yes, absolutely! DocCleaner is 100% free and open-source. There are no paid tiers, no subscription plans, and no hidden features locked behind paywalls.

Which web browsers are supported?

DocCleaner works on all Chromium-based desktop browsers, including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, and Cốc Cốc on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Does DocCleaner collect my personal browsing data?

Never. DocCleaner operates strictly client-side within your browser. It does not contain any analytics tracking code, does not monitor your history, and communicates with zero external remote servers.

How do I avoid blank pages when printing to PDF?

Use DocCleaner's floating action button rather than pressing Ctrl+P directly. DocCleaner auto-scrolls and preloads every single page asset into memory first, ensuring all images and text spans are 100% rendered before opening the Chrome print dialog.
